# Development Rules

1. Build one phase at a time.
2. Do not skip verification.
3. Do not fabricate data.
4. Do not fabricate model performance.
5. Do not introduce paid services without approval.
6. Keep the website mobile-first.
7. Keep the design professional and restrained.
8. Prefer simple architecture over unnecessary complexity.
9. Document important decisions.
10. Commit meaningful changes to GitHub.
11. Keep the repository as the permanent project memory.
12. When uncertain about a current service, API, policy or pricing detail, verify it before implementation.
